*Development* refers to the systematic process of designing, coding,
testing, and maintaining software applications. This topic encompasses
various methodologies, tools, and practices used in the creation of
software, including Agile, Waterfall, and DevOps. It highlights the
importance of collaboration among developers, project managers, and
stakeholders to deliver high-quality software that meets user needs.
Development covers a wide range of activities from initial concept and
requirements gathering to deployment and ongoing maintenance.
