Julia Sets are mathematical objects relating to the field of complex
dynamics. In general, Julia sets are studied in parallel to Fatou sets,
as they are complementary sets defined from a complex function. To be
specific, in a metric space latexmath:[(X,d)], a Fatou set of a map
latexmath:[f: X \to X] is the maximal open subset of latexmath:[X] on
which the family of iterates latexmath:[\lbrace f^n \rbrace] is
equicontinuous, and the Julia set is its complement in latexmath:[X].

To approximate images of these sets, a common approach is to iterate the
function of interest over some subset of the complex plane, which often
yield beautiful fractals.
