Gradescope is a suite of services to make grading coursework and
providing feedback easier. Gradescope streamlines the tedious parts of
grading paper-based, digital, and code assignments while providing
insights into how your students are doing. Gradescope lets instructors
save time on grading so that they can do more of what matters –
teaching! With Gradescope, students receive faster and more detailed
feedback on their work, and instructors can see detailed assignment and
question analytics.
