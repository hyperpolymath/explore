*Image* refers to a depiction or representation that visually portrays
objects, scenes, or ideas. This topic covers a broad spectrum from
digital photographs and illustrations to complex graphics used in
various forms of media. It explores the creation, editing, and usage of
images in fields like photography, graphic design, advertising, and
digital art. This topic also delves into the technologies and software
that facilitate image processing and enhancement, highlighting trends
and innovations in visual media.
