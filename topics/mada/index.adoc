The Saudi Payments Network or mada (formerly SPAN) is the only and major
payment system in the kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It connects all ATM and
point of sale (POS) terminals throughout the country to a central
payment switch which in turn re-routes the financial transactions to the
card issuer (local bank, Visa, American Express or MasterCard). All
banks in Saudi Arabia are required by the Saudi Arabian Monetary Agency
(SAMA) to issue ATM cards fully compatible with the network. All
services are provided to the customer free of charge, regardless of the
ATM used, its operator, or the customer’s card issuer.
