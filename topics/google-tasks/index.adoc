*Google Tasks* lets users capture and manage to-dos anywhere in Google
Workspace or using iOS and Android apps. It began as a feature of Gmail
and Calendar before launching as a standalone app in June 2018. It
integrates directly with Google Calendar, and tasks can be created from
emails in Gmail.
