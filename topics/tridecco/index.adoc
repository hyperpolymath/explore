An multiplayer board game using 13-sided pieces, based on Einstein’s
shape discovered in 2023. Players try to get rid of all of their pieces.
Forming a hexagon of the same color, using two-color Einstein shapes
will give the player another turn. Simultaneously they try to prevent
their opponents from forming hexagons.
