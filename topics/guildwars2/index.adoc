Guild Wars 2 is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game
developed by ArenaNet and published by NCSOFT. Set in the fantasy world
of Tyria, the game follows the re-emergence of Destiny’s Edge, a
disbanded guild dedicated to fighting the Elder Dragons, a Lovecraftian
species that has seized control of Tyria in the time since the original
Guild Wars. The game takes place in a persistent world with a story that
progresses in instanced environments.
