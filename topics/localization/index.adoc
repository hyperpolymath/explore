*Software localization* (also spelled "`localisation`", often
abbreviated to *l10n* — this is a numeronym, where the "`10`" stands for
the 10 letters between the first letter "`l`" and the last letter "`n`")
means translation of a software interface and messages to another
language plus adaptation of some formats (e.g. measures, dates and
currency) plus adaptation to local cultures.
