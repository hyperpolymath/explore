Twitch is a live streaming video platform owned by Twitch Interactive, a
subsidiary of Amazon. Introduced in June 2011 as a spin-off of the
general-interest streaming platform, Justin.tv, the site primarily
focuses on video game live streaming, including broadcasts of eSports
competitions, in addition to music broadcasts, creative content, and
more recently, "`in real life`" streams. Content on the site can be
viewed either live or via video on demand.
