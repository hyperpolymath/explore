Global climate change refers to the rise of earth’s temperature, caused
by human factors. It originates from the greenhouse effect of certain
gases in our atmosphere like carbon dioxide (CO2) or methane (CH4) that
block the escaping heat. The concentration of these gases has risen
dramatically by human impact since the mid of the 20th century, with the
burning of fossil fuels (oil and gas) and deforestation being main
causes of this rise. The observed and expected effects include more and
longer periods of draught, wildfires and an increased number of extreme
weather events.
