For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology (FIRST) is an
international youth organization that operates the FIRST Robotics
Competition, FIRST LEGO League, FIRST Lego League Jr., and FIRST Tech
Challenge competitions. Founded by Dean Kamen and Woodie Flowers in
1989, its expressed goal is to develop ways to inspire students in
engineering and technology fields. Its philosophy is expressed by the
organization as coopertition and gracious professionalism.
