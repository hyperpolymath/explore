*FFmpeg* is a free and open source software project consisting of a
suite of libraries and programs for handling video, audio, and other
multimedia files and streams. At its core is the command-line `+ffmpeg+`
tool itself, designed for processing of video and audio files. It is
widely used for format transcoding, basic editing (trimming and
concatenation), video scaling, video post-production effects and
standards compliance (SMPTE, ITU).
