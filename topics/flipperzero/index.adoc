The Flipper Zero is a hardware security module for your pocket. It is a
small, open source, hacker-friendly device that allows you to store and
manage your passwords, secrets, and keys in a secure way. It is based on
the STM32F411CEU6 microcontroller and has a 2.4” color display, a
microSD card slot, a USB-C connector, and a 3.7V 500mAh battery. The
Flipper Zero is a part of the Flipper family of devices, which also
includes the Flipper Desktop and Flipper Cloud.
