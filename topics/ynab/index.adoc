YNAB stands for "`You Need a Budget,`" a personal finance app and
methodology that helps people manage money using a zero-based budgeting
system. Every dollar is assigned a category within your plan. YNAB
focuses on building better spending and saving habits, rather than
analyzing all financial data.

Learn more at https://www.ynab.com/[ynab.com] or explore the
https://api.ynab.com/[YNAB API].
