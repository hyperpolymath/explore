The Global Game Jam (GGJ) is an annual distributed game jam. Inspired by
the Nordic Game Jam, and created by Susan Gold, Ian Schreiber and Gorm
Lai, originally developed under the International Game Developers
Association Education SIG to bring together the elements of creativity,
collaboration and experimentation. At each site, participants gather to
develop ideas, form small groups, create new, creative, innovative
games, and present them to their peers and the global community, all in
a limited time span.
