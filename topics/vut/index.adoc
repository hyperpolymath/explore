Vysoké učení technické v Brně (VUT), or Brno University of Technology
(BUT) in English, is a Czech public vocational university located in
Brno, Czechia, offering technical, natural scientific, economic and
artistic disciplines with eight faculties and three institutes. Founded
in 1899, VUT is one of the largest universities in the Czech Republic,
cooperating with numerous research institutions and consistently ranking
in QS World University Rankings and EECA University Rankings.
