Entity resolution (also known as data matching, data linkage, record
linkage, and many other terms) is the task of finding entities in a
dataset that refer to the same entity across different data sources
(e.g., data files, books, websites, and databases). Entity resolution is
necessary when joining different data sets based on entities that may or
may not share a common identifier (e.g., database key, URI, National
identification number), which may be due to differences in record shape,
storage location, or curator style or preference.
