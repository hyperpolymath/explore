Drupal is a free and opensource web content management system (CMS)
written in PHP and distributed under the GNU General Public
License.Drupal provides an opensource backend framework for at least 14%
of the top 10,000 websites worldwide and 1.2% of the top 10 million
websitesranging from personal blogs to corporate, political, and
government sites.Systems also use Drupal for knowledge management and
for business collaboration.
