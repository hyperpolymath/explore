Flow blockchain is a scalable, fast, and developer-friendly blockchain
platform designed to support high-throughput applications, such as games
and digital collectibles, without the need for layer-two solutions or
sharding.

Unlike traditional blockchain architectures, Flow utilizes a
https://flow.com/primer#primer-how-flow-works[unique multi-node design],
where different types of nodes perform distinct tasks - execution,
verification, collection, and consensus. This design allows for
significant improvements in scalability while preserving
decentralization and security.
